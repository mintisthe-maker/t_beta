# Torrentath Campaign Console — Beta Term

**Build v283 · 2026-08-17.** This page ships **beside** the zip, never inside it.

---

You have been given an **unlisted, unfinished build** of the Torrentath Campaign
Console. Six things, and they are the whole agreement:

1. **This build only.** The term covers the build named above. It does not carry
   forward to any other version, and it is not a licence to the finished product.

2. **It will be superseded.** A newer build replaces this one. When it arrives,
   the old one stops being the thing anyone is talking about.

3. **Do not redistribute it.** Not the zip, not the HTML, not the images, not
   modified, not unmodified — to anyone, including other testers. If someone
   should have it, say so and they will be sent it.

4. **Private tables only. Feedback anywhere.**
   - **Run it at your own table** — as many sessions as you like.
   - **No streaming it, and no posting pictures of it.** The artifact stays off
     the public internet until it is listed.
   - **Say whatever you like about it, publicly, to anyone.** Your opinion is
     yours. Praise it, pan it, argue about it. The restriction is on the
     *product*, never on what you think of it.
   - **Screenshots sent TO US are wanted, not merely allowed.** A picture in the
     Discord is the single most useful thing you can send. The ban is on
     *posting* them, not on taking them.

5. **The channel is the Discord.** `https://discord.gg/78hSzda5bf` — that is
   where reports go, and it is the only place this link appears. The product
   itself deliberately carries **no URL of any kind**; it says *quote the build
   line and send it wherever you got this build*, and this is where you got it.

6. **Your campaign may not survive an update, and the build will tell you.**
   From v275 every saved thing carries a version stamp. When a new build meets a
   save written in a shape it does not recognise — **including every save that
   exists today, which predates stamping** — it **refuses to load that piece and
   says so** rather than half-loading it. **Nothing is deleted.** The data is
   renamed and kept on your machine, the console's **↺ Resets** page lists every
   refused piece by name, and an **Adopt at current shape** button next to each
   one tells the build to trust it. Opening the older build also gives it back.

   *A build that refuses a save may be wrong about it, so it may not destroy it.*

**What this is not.** It is not a sale, not a subscription, and there is nothing
to pay. It is not a promise that the finished product will contain any particular
